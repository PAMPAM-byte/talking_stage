Reply processing uses separate claims and commits, expiring leases, at most three explicit attempts, atomic project/user daily budget reservations and one persisted output. Completion rejects stale ownership/access, generation, archive, memory/profile, direction/configuration and paused eligibility. Conservative daily usage totals survive conversation deletion.
