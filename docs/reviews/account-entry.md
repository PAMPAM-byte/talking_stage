# Account-entry design refinement

16 October 2026. The founder requested a professional, modern and sleek redesign of registration, sign-in and recovery after accepting vertical centering.

The direction uses TalkingStage red `#CF2445`, white `#FFFFFF`, blush `#FFF1F3`, ink `#242126` and muted `#6C666E`. Manrope carries forms and headings; DM Serif Display accents the character-side headline and conversation clue. Desktop pairs a quiet portrait panel with a vertically centered form. Mobile prioritizes the form and hides the editorial panel. The signature is an existing fictional character's approved conversation clue, rather than invented testimonials, activity or account benefits.

`AccountPage` supplies shared presentation for both connected and mock flows. The existing approved public Amara portrait is a marketing illustration, not a change to cast publication or account preferences. The image has a truthful failure fallback and fictional/AI-generated labels. The three pages retain their existing validation, loading, errors, recovery acknowledgement, age requirements and account operations. Registration's heading is shortened to “Create your account.” Password visibility is a local control that preserves input, labels, autocomplete and validation; it does not persist credentials.

The scoped `auth-*` rules are owned by `components/account-presentation.css` and loaded from the ordered global entry. The current Turbopack CSS-loader setup does not supply CSS Module exports reliably, so explicit page/component prefixes prevent leakage into other routes. Later onboarding and settings retain their existing presentation. Automatic margins center the account layout when space permits; compact screens scroll normally.

The founder subsequently requested the same treatment for `/onboarding/age`. It now shares the editorial shell and portrait, with an 18+ introduction and a full-width checkbox card. Selection, keyboard focus, disabled Continue and underage feedback retain the existing declaration semantics; this remains self-declaration, not independent age verification. Direct connected-page checks passed at 320×480, 390×844, 768×900 and 1440×1000, including unchecked/checked states and horizontal fit. Scoped lint and TypeScript passed. [Mobile age page](./account-entry/age-390.png) and [desktop age page](./account-entry/age-1440.png) capture the presentation.

Both targeted mock browser journeys passed after the age update: the full adult registration/onboarding/refresh/sign-out journey and underage blocking with sign-in/recovery regression. No real declaration or account operation was submitted during the connected-page layout checks.

Validation: scoped ESLint and TypeScript passed. The final isolated production build and production browser/privacy/tool-exclusion checks passed. All eight existing onboarding/theme browser cases passed across the initial run and targeted reruns. The first run exposed an outdated expectation that a now-hidden discovery greeting was visible; the test now checks the current discovery heading and retained name. Its multi-route screenshot check also exceeded the default timeout while the build ran; the isolated rerun passed in 33 seconds. No service behavior was changed to satisfy those checks.

Direct connected-page checks covered all three routes at 320, 390, 768, 900 and 1440px, including a short 320×480 viewport: no horizontal overflow or duplicate main landmark, password show/hide preserves input, image failure has a fallback, and reduced motion follows the existing global override. No real forms were submitted. Existing mock journeys verify registration, validation, recovery, navigation/draft preservation and sign-in. Physical-device/screen-reader and hosted email acceptance remain separate.
