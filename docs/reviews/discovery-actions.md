## Research and interpretation

- [Tinder's Rewind documentation](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115004493323-Rewind) describes the yellow arrow for taking back a swipe. Its arrow/color association informed the smaller amber Undo control.
- [Bumble's People-tab guide](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28423154479645-Using-the-People-tab) documents left/right swipe actions and the undo-arrow interaction.
- [Visual interface references](https://blog.snappymob.com/uiux-audit-bumble-vs-cmb-vs-tinder) and dating-interface image searches informed the separated circular-control composition. The specific size hierarchy, red heart, neutral cross and labels are TalkingStage design choices, not a claim that these apps share identical current UI.

The design borrows interaction vocabulary only. Meet still opens/resumes the existing AI conversation; Pass remains local browsing; Undo restores the last passed profile. No matching, subscription, boost or payment feature is added. No reference images were imported into product assets.
