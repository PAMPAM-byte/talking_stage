# Support page refinement

The founder requested a professional support page. Red `#CF2445`, white, blush `#FFF1F3`, ink `#242126`, and muted `#6C666E` maintain the shared identity, with Manrope headings/body and a restrained serif supporting line.

The signature is four actionable help topics: account/access, conversations/memory, payments/requests, and reporting/boundaries. These anchors lead to practical guidance and existing routes. Five native expandable FAQs cover recovery, memory deletion, optional requests, pending payments, and contact availability. A blush contact panel explains the unselected production support channel and makes clear that no request is submitted here.

The design deliberately avoids a search field with no useful search behavior, fabricated live-agent availability, contact details, response promises, or a nonfunctional submission form. Existing provisional product information and local-preview limitations remain visible. No backend operations are introduced.

Review covers 320, 390, 768, and 1440 pixel widths, keyboard topic navigation and focus, FAQ opening/closing, and recovery navigation. Screenshots: [mobile](./support-red-390.png), [desktop](./support-red-1440.png).

TypeScript, targeted ESLint, and both browser checks passed. The desktop screenshot was visually reviewed for spacing, clear topic hierarchy, and consistency with the shared landing identity.
