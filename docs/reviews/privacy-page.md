# Privacy page refinement

The founder requested a professional Privacy page. It follows the established red `#CF2445`, white, blush `#FFF1F3`, ink `#242126`, and muted `#6C666E` palette with Manrope text and a restrained DM Serif Display supporting line.

The signature is a three-part privacy overview, followed by an indexed document with seven sections. The copy distinguishes account preferences, conversation history, saved memories, deletion options, payment/report records, and storage in the local preview. A blush note emphasizes that deleting conversations and clearing saved memories are separate choices. Existing links lead to memory controls and account deletion; no new backend behavior is introduced.

The draft notice and unresolved controller/provider/retention/contact details remain visible. No effective date, privacy contact, legal promise, or production storage claim is invented. Mock storage descriptions are explicitly limited to the local preview.

Review covers responsive rendering at 320, 390, 768, and 1440 pixels, keyboard section navigation, real settings-link targets, payment information navigation, and entry from Terms. Screenshots: [mobile](./privacy-red-390.png), [desktop](./privacy-red-1440.png).

TypeScript, targeted ESLint, and both browser checks passed. The rendered mobile page was visually reviewed for spacing, hierarchy, and legibility.
