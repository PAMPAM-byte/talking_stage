# Discover refinement · 14 October 2026

The founder requested a less cluttered, professional Discover page and online inspiration. [Tinder's FAQ](https://tinder.com/en-GB/faq/) documents simple left/right swipe actions; [Bumble's interest-badge guide](https://bumble.com/the-buzz/bumble-badges) emphasizes quickly readable profile details. These inform the portrait/action hierarchy without importing human matching, paid filters, distances, or compatibility scores.

Shared red/white/blush colours and Manrope/serif typography remain. The signature is one portrait paired with one conversation-clue panel. Biography and interests move into an accessible expandable section. Compact gender/filter controls replace competing explanatory blocks; fictional AI/adult disclosures remain on the card and selection line. Mobile and tablet card heights reserve space for action buttons and bottom navigation. Desktop uses a quieter two-column portrait/clue composition.

Swipe, keyboard alternatives, Undo, filter continuity, Meet-to-chat, paused-chat recovery, and heart feedback are retained. Changes apply to shared mock/connected presentation without new backend operations.

TypeScript and targeted ESLint passed. Five existing deck checks passed after correcting mobile/tablet card heights to clear fixed navigation. Responsive evidence: [mobile](./discover-refined-390.png), [desktop](./discover-refined-1440.png). The mobile rendering was visually reviewed. Live hosted access was not tested.
