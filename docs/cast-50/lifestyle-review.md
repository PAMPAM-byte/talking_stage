## Six-profile expansion and research

The founder requested 16 featured young adult baddie/big-girl personalities, not six new identities on top of the 50-person cast. The ten baddie-tagged women remain; six big-girl identities complete the group. Each has different tastes, confidence and conversational illustrations. Filters describe established personality rather than changing a character to match a user.

The six new main portraits show Kiki at public stadium stands, Naomi on a Bali filming trip, Hauwa in a fabric showroom, Damilola at a rooftop dinner, Zainab at a Marrakech design hotel and Nimi at a Zanzibar resort. All were generated with the built-in image tool using their existing adult portraits as identity references and visually inspected before publication. Old images are retained. Travel scenes are past fictional experiences, not live locations. Kiki does not claim FIFA employment, official credentials, access to players or live scores.

Career research informed practical duties rather than stereotypes about appearance or wealth:
